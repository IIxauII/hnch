# robots.txt for https://hnch.dev (RFC 9309)
#
# Room pages and invite links carry a room code and only mean something to the
# people in that room. The relay paths are an API, not pages. Everything else
# (the start page, icons, licenses) may be crawled.
#
# Content signals (https://contentsignals.org/, IETF draft
# draft-romm-aipref-contentsignals): what crawled pages may be used for.
# search: a search index with links and short excerpts. ai-input: feeding
# pages into AI models to answer a question (grounding, RAG). ai-train:
# training or fine-tuning AI models.
#
# A crawler follows only the most specific User-agent group that names it, so
# every group repeats the Content-Signal line and the rules of the * group.
# packages/client/scripts/robots.test.ts checks this.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: ai-train=yes, search=yes, ai-input=yes
Disallow: /lobby
Disallow: /estimating
Disallow: /join
Disallow: /rooms
Disallow: /ws/
Disallow: /health
Allow: /
